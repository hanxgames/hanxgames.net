Website Privacy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What this website does not do

hanxgames.net uses no cookies, ads or trackers and does not identify you. Your language choice is kept only in your browser's local storage.

Visit totals

To know how many people visit, the site counts page views as daily totals in our Firebase database (Google): the page, the page language, the kind of site you came from (for example "Google" or "Reddit", taken only from that site's domain name), the device type (phone or computer) and clicks on store links. No cookie, identifier or IP address is stored with these totals, and nothing is sent if your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on.

Store links and embedded content

Our Google Play links carry a campaign tag (for example utm_source=hanxgames.net) so that Google Play can show us, as a total, how many installs came from this site. The tag contains no personal data.

The ReBirth page shows itch.io's purchase box for the PC version. It loads from itch.io when you scroll to it; from then on itch.io may process data such as your IP address and device information, and may store data on your device, under its own privacy policy.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. These providers, and Google when receiving the visit totals, may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure their services.

Our games

Each game has its own privacy policy, linked from its store page and inside the game.
