Three maps · three disasters · three strategies

Each province breaks in its own way

EarthquakeTarsel

Aftershocks bring down unreinforced buildings. Set up tent cities, search & rescue and seismic retrofits.

FloodKesra Delta

The river rises every season. Build levees, pumping stations and stilt houses, then farm the fertile silt.

DroughtVarzan Plateau

Every district has its own water stress. Drill wells, build cisterns, run water tankers and plant a green belt.

Rebuild · Command · Win hearts

Hard power, soft power, and the tough calls between

Rebuild

40 initiatives: rubble clearing, housing, water, power grids, clinics, schools, markets, local councils and map-specific projects. Watch towns come back to life.

Command your forces

Infantry, mechanized units, gendarmerie, special forces, engineers and local militias. Patrol, assault or guard, but civilian casualties cost legitimacy.

Air power

Recon drones, armed drones, attack helicopters and jets, with 7 research upgrades.

Fight a shadow

Insurgent cells hide, recruit, sabotage and escalate. You need intelligence just to see them: informant networks, checkpoints and patrols.

Win through peace

Reach 100% legitimacy and the insurgents come to the table. Sign the peace deal and win the campaign.

Crisis desk

7 short, high-stakes missions with 3-star ratings, from a cholera outbreak to a cracking dam.

A living world

At night, the lights tell the story

  • Day-night cycle: stable districts glow, contested ones flicker, insurgent-held ones go dark.
  • Aid convoys, election night, a donor conference, press conferences and harvest festivals with fireworks.
  • About 80 dynamic events, 30 policies and a monthly newspaper that writes your story.
  • A time-lapse of your whole campaign, plus unit voice lines and radio chatter in English and Turkish.
