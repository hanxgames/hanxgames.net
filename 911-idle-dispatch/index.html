    About the game

    A 911 dispatcher simulator for police, fire and ambulance fans

    911: Idle Dispatch puts you in the chair of an emergency dispatcher. Answer 911 calls, read each situation and send the right police, fire or ambulance (EMS) units across a detailed city map.

    Traffic accidents, building fires, robberies and medical emergencies arrive in real time. Your teams get tired, resources are limited and public opinion reacts on social media, so every call is a decision. Earn rewards, upgrade your fleet and grow a small dispatch center into a citywide command operation, with idle progress that continues while you are away.

    Play on real-world maps, in your own language, online or offline. If you like police games, firefighter games, ambulance games and emergency management simulators, this is your next shift.

    FAQ

    911: Idle Dispatch – FAQ

    Is 911: Idle Dispatch free?

    Yes. It is free to play on Android and iOS. It includes ads, and an optional one-time purchase removes forced ads.

    Can I play 911: Idle Dispatch offline?

    Yes. You can play without an internet connection.

    What do you do in a 911 dispatch game?

    You answer emergency calls, decide which units are needed, dispatch police officers, firefighters and paramedics, and manage your teams, upgrades and public reaction during each shift.

    Does the game use real maps?

    Yes. You play on real-world locations, from your own street to cities around the world.
