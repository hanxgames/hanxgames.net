FAQ

Questions before you buy

Which game engines do the packs work with?

Any engine that reads glTF, FBX or OBJ: Unity, Godot, Unreal Engine, Blender, three.js and more.

Can I use the models in a commercial game?

Yes. Use them in unlimited personal and commercial projects, including games, apps, videos and client work, without attribution. Only reselling or redistributing the assets themselves is not allowed.

Are the models low poly and mobile-friendly?

Yes. Most models have a few hundred triangles, the largest buildings stay under 3,000, and one shared texture keeps draw calls low on phones and tablets.

Do the packs fit together?

Yes. Every HanX Assets pack uses the same palette, scale and 8 m grid, so a traffic street, a school and a gas station can sit side by side in one scene.

Where do I buy and download the packs?

On itch.io. Right after purchase you can download a ZIP with every format, and you can download updated versions of the pack later at no extra cost.
