DreadMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how DreadMate, made by HanX Games, handles data in its Android version (from Google Play) and its iOS version (from the App Store). Where the two versions differ, each section says so. For the hanxgames.net website, see the website privacy page.

What the game does not use

DreadMate shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. It does not ask you for your name, email address or phone number, does not use your advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Your progress on your device

Your progress (unlocks, upgrades, statistics and settings) is stored in a save file on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game. On iOS, the file can also be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device if you have turned that on.

Guest account and cloud save (Google Firebase)

The game uses Firebase, a set of services from Google LLC, for its online features. When you first open the game while online, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous guest account for your installation. This account has a random user ID; it is not linked to your name, email address, Google account or Apple Account unless you choose to sign in as described below.

Under this user ID the game keeps a copy of your save file in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud), together with a progress score and the time of the last update. This cloud save lets the game restore your progress if you reinstall it or play on another device with the same Google Play Games or Game Center account. The game also downloads its settings from Firebase Remote Config, which can adjust game balance and texts without an update.

Optional sign-in: Google Play Games (Android) and Game Center (iOS)

From Settings > Account you can sign in with Google Play Games on Android or with Game Center on iOS, but you do not have to. If you do, Google or Apple confirms your player identity to Firebase, your guest account is linked to it, and the game receives your player display name to show it in the account panel. This is what lets your cloud save follow you to another device. Google handles Play Games data under the Google Privacy Policy and Apple handles Game Center data under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

Both versions use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played so that we can improve it and balance its difficulty. It records game events (signing in, starting and ending a run, levelling up and taking a gambit), an app instance ID that Firebase creates for your installation, your Firebase user ID, and device information such as device model, operating system, language, the game version and your approximate country, which Google derives from your IP address. Analytics does not collect your advertising ID in DreadMate and the data is not used for advertising. Data linked to the app instance ID is deleted automatically after the retention period we have set, which is at most 14 months. See Firebase privacy and data processing information.

Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

Both versions use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports. A report can include your device model, operating system version, the game version, where the crash or error happened in the game's code, short game log lines (for example the weapon you were using), your Firebase user ID and a Crashlytics installation ID. We use these reports only to find and fix bugs and keep the game stable.

How we use and share data

We use the data above to run the game's online features (cloud save and sign-in), to fix bugs and to improve the game, on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving it. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone other than Google, which provides Firebase to us as a data processor under the Firebase data processing terms. Google may process the data in countries other than yours.

Google Play and the App Store

Buying, downloading and updating the game through Google Play or the App Store is governed by that store's own terms and privacy policy, not by this one. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It contains fantasy combat between stylized chess-piece characters.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and cloud data yourself in the game: open Settings > Account and tap "Delete my account and cloud data" twice. This deletes your cloud save and your Firebase account; the progress on your device is reset as well. Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device but not your cloud save. You can also email us at the address below to ask for deletion of your data, including Analytics and Crashlytics data, or for a copy of it; please tell us your platform, your device model and, if you signed in, your Google Play Games or Game Center player name. Game Center and Google Play Games data itself is managed from your Apple Account or Google account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
