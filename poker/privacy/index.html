Hantro: Poker Roguelike Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how Hantro: Poker Roguelike, made by HanX Games, handles data in its iOS version (from the App Store) and its Android version (from Google Play). Where the two versions differ, each section says so. For the hanxgames.net website, see the website privacy page.

What the game does not do

The game has no account to create and does not ask for your email address, phone number or real name. It does not use your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos. On iOS it does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or its ad partners.

Your progress on your device

Your runs, unlocks, statistics and settings are stored in a save file on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game. On iOS, the file can be included in your own iCloud or computer backup if you have turned that on.

Global leaderboard (Google Firebase)

When you are online, the game uses Firebase, a set of services from Google LLC, to keep a global leaderboard. Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account for your installation with a random user ID. When you finish a run with a new best score, the game sends the leaderboard name you chose, your score, the round you reached, a random ID created by the game for your installation and the time to Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud). Your leaderboard name and score are visible to other players. You can change your name in Settings at any time.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob between runs and offers optional rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data such as your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, an app-scoped device identifier (on Android, also the Android Advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings), ad interactions and diagnostic data, in order to show, measure and protect ads. In the EU, the UK and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice in Settings > Privacy options. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ad-free purchase

The one-time ad-free purchase is processed by the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We never see your payment details. The game receives only a confirmation that the purchase belongs to you, which it uses to turn ads off and to restore the purchase on a new device.

Android only: Google Play Games and reminders

On Android, the game can sign you in to Google Play Games silently to submit scores and achievements and to suggest your Play Games name for the leaderboard. It can also show local reminder notifications if you allow notifications; these are created on your device and send no data. The iOS version does not use Game Center and does not send notifications.

How we use and share data

We use the data above only to run the leaderboard, to show ads and to provide the ad-free purchase. We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google (Firebase and AdMob) and with Apple or Google for purchases, as described above. Google may process the data in countries other than yours.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes the data on your device. To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us at the address below with your leaderboard name and platform. Data collected by Google for ads is managed under Google's policies; on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
