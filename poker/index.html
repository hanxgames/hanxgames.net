Fill the lantern

Every round has a target: a lantern that fills with the light of your score. Reach it and the road goes on, miss it and the run ends.

  1. Pick up to 5 of your 8 cards and play a poker hand. There are 10 hand types, and each one levels up.
  2. You get 4 hands and 4 discards per round. Your jokers fire from left to right and the mult climbs.
  3. Win the round, visit the peddler’s stall and shape your deck. Every third round a henchman blocks the road.

What’s inside

The Devil deals. You break the deck.

Short runs, big combos and that “one more round” itch, in a dark, warm world with a rival who is funny but never a pushover.

72 jokers with attitude

Every joker is a character with a job: conditions, growth, money engines, and some that ask a price. Five slots on your shelf, endless ways to fill them.

The Devil and his henchmen

Every third round one of 13 henchmen blocks the road with a rule of its own: a blind tailor, an ink octopus, a thieving magpie. At the end, the Devil himself sits down.

Devil’s Road

A branching map with four suit-coloured paths, a final showdown and permanent powers. Prestige lets you walk it again, harder.

Tension and Overflow

Henchman rounds and the last hand of a round are Tense: the light dims and your jokers react. Beat the target by a wide margin and the spare light turns into sparks and coins.

Teas, tricks and card marks

Teas level up your hands and single-use tricks bend the rules. Tea stains, wax drips, burns and ink blots mark your cards for good, and you can see them on the card.

Glazes and the card album

Rare glazes such as Moonlight and Gold Foil shine on cards and jokers. Collect what you find in a leather-bound album and swap looks with 5 card sets.

About the game

A poker roguelike deckbuilder for fans of Balatro-style card games

Hantro: Poker Roguelike turns poker hands into a roguelike run. Play pairs, straights and flushes, level up your hands with teas, and let your jokers multiply the score round after round. Late in a run the targets climb into the millions, and the longest runs push into the billions.

Between rounds you visit the peddler’s stall for jokers, teas, tricks and surprise boxes, always hunting for the next synergy. Henchman rounds bring their own rules, and the Devil waits at the end of the road. Clear a branch of the Devil’s Road to earn a permanent power, then prestige it and walk it again.

Season 2 rebuilds the game in a warm, dark style: a lantern-lit inn, rain on the window and characters with sharp grins. It plays offline, and a Season 2 leaderboard tracks the best runs. Hantro is for entertainment only: no real money, no prizes.

FAQ

Hantro: Poker Roguelike – FAQ

Is Hantro: Poker Roguelike free?

Yes, it is free to download on Google Play. The game shows ads, and a one-time purchase removes them. You can also continue a run once by watching an optional rewarded ad.

What is new in Season 2?

Hantro was rebuilt from scratch: new art and effects, 72 jokers, 13 henchmen and the Devil, the Devil’s Road, and new systems such as Tension, Overflow, card marks, glazes, teas and tricks.

Will I keep my Season 1 progress?

Yes. Your Season 1 save carries over automatically when you update. Season 1 high scores stay on their own honor roll, and Season 2 has a fresh leaderboard.

Is it real-money gambling?

No. Hantro is a strategy card game for entertainment only. There is no real money and there are no prizes.

Can I play this poker roguelike offline?

Yes. Hantro plays offline, so you can play anywhere without an internet connection. You only need a connection for the online leaderboard.

Which languages does the game support?

The game is available in English and Turkish.

Is it like Balatro?

If you enjoy Balatro-style poker roguelikes, you will feel at home: you score poker hands, collect jokers and build combos. The rest is Hantro’s own: the inn, the Devil and his henchmen, the Devil’s Road, teas, tricks and card marks.
