Hantro: Poker Roguelike Support

Need help with Hantro: Poker Roguelike? Write to us at hanxdestek@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days. Please tell us your device model, whether you play on iPhone/iPad or Android, and the game version (Settings, at the bottom).

Restoring the ad-free purchase

The ad-free purchase is tied to your Apple Account or Google account. On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the game while online; your purchase is restored automatically. If it is not, open the Ad-free sign in the inn and tap Restore purchase.

Saves and progress

Your runs, unlocks and settings are saved on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them, so we cannot recover a lost save. On iPhone and iPad, your progress is included in your device backup if you use iCloud or computer backups.

Leaderboard name

The name you choose the first time you open the game is shown on the global leaderboard. You can change it at any time in Settings. Names that are offensive may be removed from the leaderboard.

Ads

The game shows ads between runs and offers optional rewarded ads to continue a run. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings > Privacy options.

Is this a gambling game?

No. Hantro is a single-player card strategy game. Poker hands are only its scoring rules: there is no betting, no real money and no prizes.
