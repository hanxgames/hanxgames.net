Deploy · Strike · Hold the front

A command post in your pocket

Frontaxis is an operational strategy game in real time. You do not control soldiers one by one: you command a brigade, and you read the battle the way a real headquarters does.

Real geography

85 real-terrain maps: elevation, rivers, bridges, forests, roads and towns come from the real world, with contour lines and a military grid.

Campaign

Black Sea, Baltic and Gulf crises on a theatre map. Move your corps, plan attacks and take the target cities in 40 days.

Quick battle

Pick a map, your nation and your opponent. Attack or defend, at four difficulty levels.

Army deck

Build your own brigade with 50 activation points: tanks, mechanised infantry, artillery, air defence, drones and helicopters.

Realistic combat

Terrain, range, armour and ammunition all count. Counter-battery fire, smoke and precision munitions change the fight.

Drones and electronic warfare

Recon drones, loitering munitions and FPVs. Jam enemy links and GPS, and call in air sorties.

Radio and APP-6 symbols

Voiced radio reports and NATO APP-6 military symbols: you follow the battle like a real command post.

Built for mobile

Tap a unit, tap the target. Pause whenever you like to give orders. No internet connection needed.

How a battle plays

Find them. Strike them. Take the ground.

Every engagement follows real military logic: see the enemy first, prepare with fires, then manoeuvre onto the objective.

  1. 01Recon

    A drone picks up a suspected contact. A closer look turns it into an identified tank platoon.

  2. 02Fires

    Your artillery fires on the target, then moves: enemy radar is listening for counter-battery fire.

  3. 03Manoeuvre

    Your companies cross the bridge in a wedge while the enemy is suppressed.

  4. 04Objective

    Hold the objective and the sector changes hands. The front line moves.

Every army fights its own way

Each nation brings its own vehicles, drones, aircraft and doctrine. Choose your country and command its forces.

  • Türkiye

    Drone-led recon-strike chain, strong artillery and guided rockets.

  • United States

    Multi-domain heavy brigades backed by air power and long-range precision fires.

  • United Kingdom

    Small but high-tech: find with recon and drones, strike with precision fires.

  • Germany

    Heavy armoured brigades at the heart of NATO’s land forces.

  • France

    Networked expeditionary army: wheeled manoeuvre and shoot-and-scoot artillery.

  • Poland

    Mass heavy forces with the weight of artillery and rocket launchers.

  • Greece

    Heavy mechanised brigades and layered air defence.

  • Ukraine

    Drone-centred defence with FPV and heavy bomber drones.

  • Russia

    Firepower first: massed artillery and rockets, glide bombs and FPV swarms.

  • Iran

    Dispersed mosaic defence, built on missiles and masses of drones.

  • China

    Modular combined-arms brigades and long-range precision fires.

  • Israel

    Intelligence-driven precision fire, with active protection on almost every vehicle.

Countries and geography are real. Scenarios are fiction and do not represent any real event or government.

Army deck

Your brigade, your rules

  • Spend 50 activation points across command, recon, infantry, armour, support, air defence and air units.
  • Pick each unit’s model and experience, from rookie to elite.
  • Every nation fields its own vehicles, drones and aircraft.
  • Reinforcements roll in while the battle is still running.
  • In a hurry? Auto-fill the deck or start from a ready-made mechanised, armoured or commando deck.

Real terrain

Read the map like a commander

  • Maps built from real elevation data, rivers, bridges, forests, roads and settlements.
  • Contour lines, a military grid and a scale bar, just like a real command table.
  • Line of sight comes from the terrain: hills hide you, forests and towns give cover.
  • Contacts go from suspected to detected to identified, and old reports fade with time.

About the game

A real-time military strategy game on real terrain

Frontaxis is a real-time military strategy game for Android, set in 2030. You command a brigade on 85 real-terrain maps across Europe, the Black Sea and the Middle East, where elevation, rivers, bridges, forests, roads and towns come from the real world. Twelve real nations field their own armies: Türkiye, the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Poland, Greece, Ukraine, Russia, Iran, China and Israel.

Every battle is fought like a real operation. Units spot, suspect and identify each other, orders travel down the radio net, artillery draws counter-battery fire and electronic warfare jams drones and GPS. Pause at any time, read the contour lines and give your orders with a tap.

Play the Black Sea, Baltic and Gulf crises in the campaign, set up a quick battle on the map of your choice, or build your own brigade in the army deck. Fans of wargames, real-time tactics and grand strategy get a deep war strategy game they can play offline on their phone.

FAQ

Frontaxis: frequently asked questions

What is Frontaxis?

Frontaxis is a real-time military strategy game for Android, set in 2030 across Europe, the Black Sea and the Middle East. You command a brigade of a real nation on real terrain: in the campaign, in quick battles or with your own army deck.

How do I become a Frontaxis tester?

In three steps, all with the Google account you use on Play Store: join the frontaxis-tester Google Group, tap “Become a tester” on the Google Play testing page, then install Frontaxis from Google Play.

Why should I stay in the test for 14 days?

Google Play asks new games to complete a closed test of at least 14 days before they can be released to everyone. Please keep the game installed during that time: every tester counts.

Is Frontaxis free?

Yes, Frontaxis is free to download. Everyone who takes part in the closed test also gets Frontaxis Premium as a gift when the test ends.

When will Frontaxis be released?

The public release on Google Play follows the closed test. Testers play first, and their feedback shapes the launch version.

Which countries can I command?

Twelve: Türkiye, the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France, Poland, Greece, Ukraine, Russia, Iran, China and Israel, each with its own equipment and doctrine.

Can I play Frontaxis offline?

Yes. Battles and the campaign play without an internet connection; you only need it to download the game.

Is Frontaxis available on iPhone or PC?

Not yet. Frontaxis is currently in closed testing on Android phones and tablets.

Which languages does Frontaxis support?

The game is currently in Turkish. Radio voice lines are in Turkish when you command Türkiye and in English for the other nations.

Are the scenarios real?

The countries, armies and geography are real. The scenarios are fiction and do not represent any real event or government.
