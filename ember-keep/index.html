About the game

A tower defense roguelike with heroes, spells and cards

Hantro: Ember Keep is a fast, colorful castle defense game. Enemy armies attack your keep from every side, and you hold the walls with five heroes, area spells and a catapult.

Between waves you pick roguelike power cards such as chain lightning, wildfire, arrow rain and deep frost, so every run builds a different strategy. Gold from each battle becomes permanent upgrades for heroes, walls and talents, and stars unlock new regions like the Autumn Forest and the Winter Realm, guarded by giant bosses such as the Rune Golem and the Glacier Giant.

Built for one-handed portrait play and short sessions, Ember Keep works offline and is free to play, with optional purchases and rewarded ads only when you choose to watch them.

Hantro: Ember Keep – FAQ

Is Hantro: Ember Keep free?

Yes, it is free to play. It has optional in-app purchases and rewarded ads that you only watch if you want to. There are no forced ads.

Can I play Ember Keep offline?

Yes. No internet connection is needed.

Who are the heroes in Ember Keep?

Storm Knight, Ember Mage, Frost Archer, Dwarf Gunner and Dawn Priestess, each with a different attack and special ability.

Is Ember Keep available on iPhone?

Yes. It is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and on Google Play for Android.
