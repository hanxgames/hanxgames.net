SWAT: Tactical Raids Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how SWAT: Tactical Raids, made by HanX Games, handles data in its Android version (package hantro.tower.roguelike, from Google Play) and its iOS version (from the App Store). Where the two versions differ, each section says so. For the hanxgames.net website, see the website privacy page.

What the game does not use

The game does not ask you to create an account or to give your name, email address or phone number, and it does not use your location (GPS or network location), camera, microphone or contacts.

Your progress

Your game progress is stored on your device in a local save file. Unless you use the optional Google Play Games cloud save on Android (see below), it stays on your device, and the local file is removed when you uninstall the game. On iOS, the file can also be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device if you have turned that on.

The game's server (Google Firebase)

Both the Android and the iOS version connect to the game's own server, which runs on Google Firebase and Google Cloud, services of Google LLC. The first time you open the game, it creates an anonymous guest ID for your installation through Firebase Authentication. The guest ID is not linked to your name, email address, Google account or Apple Account, and the game keeps it in its data on your device.

While you play, the game sends the following to the server under this guest ID: your platform (Android or iOS), device model and operating system version; the region of your device's language setting (for example US for English (United States)), which we use only as a rough country estimate and which is not your location; your time zone; the game's language and version; the number and length of your play sessions and when you first and last opened the game; your game progress, such as your level, experience and in-game money, tutorial step, missions started, won and failed, multiplayer (VS) statistics and which screens you use; how many purchases you have made and which permanent items you own; how many ads you have watched; your graphics quality setting, average frame rate and graphics chip (GPU) model; and whether you have allowed notifications and the status of your ad consent. It does not send your advertising ID, location, name or email address. Like any online service, the server sees your IP address while handling a connection, but the IP address is not saved in these records.

We use this data to run the game, to understand how it is played so that we can improve it (for example to see where players get stuck), to produce statistics and to find and fix bugs. We process it on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the game. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone other than Google, which hosts the server for us. The records are stored in Google Cloud's europe-west1 region (Belgium, European Union); Google may process some of the data in other countries under the Firebase data processing terms.

The game sends this data only when your device is online. There is no switch in the game to turn it off; if you object to it, write to us (see Deleting your data below). Your record stays on the server for as long as we run the game, even if you uninstall it, unless you ask us to delete it. Uninstalling removes the guest ID from your device, so a new installation gets a new guest ID and a new record.

Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The Android version also uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC, to measure how the game is used. It records game events (such as starting a play session, starting and finishing missions, tutorial steps, level-ups, purchases and ad views, including the ad revenue reported by AdMob), three properties (your level, whether you have finished the tutorial and whether you have made a purchase), an app instance ID that Firebase creates for your installation, and device information such as device model, operating system, language, the game version and your approximate country, which Google derives from your IP address. We use it to understand how players use the game, to improve it and to see how the game and its ads perform. The iOS version uses Google Analytics for Firebase from its next update onward, in the same way and under the same rules described in this section.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Analytics stores and uses its measurement ID for your installation only if you agree, in the ad consent form (see Ads below), to the purpose "Store and/or access information on a device". If you do not agree, measurement storage stays off: Analytics does not store that ID, and Google's Consent Mode may only send signals without any identifier, which Google uses in aggregated form. Outside these regions, measurement is on. When you first open the game, measurement storage stays off until the result of the consent form is known. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad consent > Privacy Options.

The same consent choices also decide whether Analytics may use the data for advertising: if you do not consent, Analytics does not use your advertising ID and does not use the data for ads or ad personalization. Where ad storage is allowed (outside these regions, or when you consent), Analytics may also record your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your Android settings. The same rules apply on iOS; in addition, if you do not allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, your IDFA is not shared with Analytics either.

Google processes Analytics data as described in How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps and in the Firebase privacy and data processing information. Analytics data linked to the app instance ID is deleted automatically after the retention period we have set, which is at most 14 months. We also copy daily totals from Analytics to the game's server (for example the number of active players per country); these totals do not identify anyone.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch them to speed something up, retry a mission or double a reward. Interstitial ads appear at the end of a mission.

To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may process data such as your device's advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA on iOS), IP address, device information and ad interactions. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad consent > Privacy Options, or by writing to us at hanxdestek@gmail.com; clearing the game's data (app data) or, on iOS, reinstalling the game also shows the consent form again the next time you open it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. We do not sell your data; ad data is processed by Google as our advertising partner. For details, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

On iOS, the game asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before it starts showing ads. If you do not allow tracking, your IDFA is not shared with AdMob; you still see ads, but they are less personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In-app purchases

The game offers in-app purchases: an ad-free pack, a starter pack and packs of in-game resources and speed-ups. On Android they are handled by Google Play Billing, and on iOS by Apple's App Store in-app purchase system. Google or Apple processes your payment details; they never reach the game or our server, and we never see your card information.

When you buy something, the game sends the purchase token (Android) or the App Store transaction details (iOS) and the product ID, together with your guest ID and the game version, to the game's server. The server checks the purchase with Google Play or Apple, which report its status, order number, country and date, and keeps a record of it so that fake or refunded purchases can be recognized. We keep purchase records for as long as we need them to handle refunds and fraud and to keep our accounts.

Notifications

On Android, the game schedules local notifications on your device (for example when construction is finished, an officer has healed or the daily operation is ready), and uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to send announcement notifications, which uses a device token. On Android 13 and later the game asks for notification permission, and you can turn notifications off in the game settings or in your system settings. The iOS version does not send notifications.

Google Play Games Services (Android, optional)

You can sign in to Google Play Games Services from the game, but you do not have to. If you do, Google provides your player ID and player name to the game. Cloud save (saved games) keeps your progress in your Google account so you can restore it on another device. Achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Google Play Games, and on leaderboards your player name may be visible to other players. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy, and you manage it from your Google account and the Play Games settings.

Game Center (iOS, optional)

On iOS, if you are already signed in to Apple's Game Center on your device, the game connects to it quietly, without showing a sign-in screen; if you are not, you can sign in from the game settings, but you do not have to. Apple then provides your Game Center player ID and display name to the game, which uses them only on your device: to show that you are connected and to suggest your call sign in the multiplayer mode. The iOS version does not use Game Center cloud saves, achievements or leaderboards. Apple handles Game Center data under the Apple Privacy Policy, and you can sign out in Settings > Game Center.

Crash and error reports

Both versions send their own error reports to the game's server when an error occurs in the game, or when the game did not close properly the last time it ran. A report does not contain your guest ID. It contains the error message and where in the game's code it happened, the last lines of the game's own log (with IDs and tokens removed), the screen that was open, how long the game had been running, the game version (and the previous version after an update), your platform, operating system, device model and graphics chip (GPU). We use these reports only to find and fix bugs, and they are deleted automatically 90 days after they are sent.

In addition, the Android version uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service from Google, to receive crash and error reports. A report can include your device model, operating system version, the game version, the crash stack trace, the last game events before the crash (such as a mission starting) and a Crashlytics installation ID. We use it only to find and fix bugs and keep the game stable. We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising. Google processes it as described in the Firebase privacy and data processing information. The iOS version does not use Crashlytics; Apple may pass crash reports on to us only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iOS settings.

Google Play and the App Store

Downloading and updating the game through Google Play or the App Store is governed by that store's own terms and privacy policy, not by this one.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and its ads are not marked as child-directed. It contains weapon violence shown with unrealistic low-poly characters.

Deleting your data

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Google Play Games data (cloud save, achievements, leaderboards) is managed from your Google account, and Game Center data from your Apple Account. Your record on the game's server is not deleted when you uninstall the game. To ask us to delete it, to object to its use or to get a copy of it, or to ask us to delete Crashlytics data, email us at the address below. The game does not show your guest ID, so please tell us your platform, your device model and the approximate date you installed the game and, if you made a purchase, the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt. We will delete the records we can match to you, and we will tell you if we cannot find a reliable match. We may keep purchase records where we need them for accounting, refunds or legal obligations. Error reports are deleted automatically after 90 days, and Analytics data at the end of its retention period.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
