Real-time squad tactics

Pause, plan, then go loud

This is real-time strategy with a tactical pause. Stop the action whenever you need to think, give new orders, then let the clock run again.

  • PLAN mode: freeze time and give each team a route of waypoints and door actions.
  • Go-codes A, B and C: breach several doors at the very same moment.
  • Line of sight: you only see what your officers see. An unexplored room stays unknown until you go in.

About the game

A tactical SWAT game for strategy fans

SWAT: Tactical Raids is a top-down police tactics game for Android. You lead an elite SWAT team through raids, hostage rescues, drug busts, bomb disposals and arrest warrants, planning each breach and clearing every building room by room.

The squad tactics play out in real time, but a tactical pause and PLAN mode let you stop the clock, set routes and door actions for each team and launch a coordinated breach with go-codes. Line of sight keeps every unexplored room a mystery, so scouting and flashbangs matter.

Back at headquarters you upgrade facilities, recruit and train officers, and equip them with weapons, armor, less-lethal tools and a K9 police dog. Score rewards arrests over kills, and every mission is graded from S to F.

FAQ

SWAT: frequently asked questions

What kind of game is SWAT: Tactical Raids?

It is a top-down tactical strategy game where you command a police SWAT team in real time. You breach doors, clear buildings, arrest suspects and rescue hostages, then upgrade your headquarters between missions.

Is SWAT: Tactical Raids free?

Yes. It is free to download on Google Play. It has optional in-app purchases and ads: optional rewarded videos and interstitial ads between missions, which a one-time purchase removes.

Is it real-time or turn-based?

Real-time, with a tactical pause. You can stop the action at any moment to give orders, or use PLAN mode to set routes and breach several doors at once with go-codes.

Which devices can play it?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play. It is not on iPhone or iPad. On lower-end devices you can turn shadows off in Settings.

Do I need a Google Play Games account?

No. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. It unlocks achievements and leaderboards and backs up your career to the cloud.

Can I play SWAT offline?

Yes. Missions and the headquarters work without an internet connection. Cloud backup, leaderboards, ads and purchases need a connection.

Does SWAT: Tactical Raids have multiplayer?

Yes, as a beta. Multiplayer has 4v4 attack and defense rounds in ranked and quick matches, with 16 operators, costumes and gear. It is separate from the career mode.

Which languages does the game support?

Seven: English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French and Russian.
