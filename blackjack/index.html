About the game

Blackjack meets roguelike deckbuilding

Hantro: BlackJack Roguelike is a strategy card game that takes the rules of 21 and turns them into a roguelike run. Hit, stand, split and double down as usual, but every hand is now shaped by the cards, Jokers and upgrades you collect along the way.

Build a deck around powerful Joker effects, defeat bosses with unique mechanics, buy and arrange new casino tables and climb the global leaderboards. Short sessions and endless replayability make it perfect for quick breaks.

The game uses virtual chips only, so it is pure strategy and fun with no real-money gambling. Download it free on Android, iPhone and iPad.
