About the game

An esports manager game for MOBA fans

Moba Manager: Esports Coach lets you run a professional MOBA esports team without touching the controls. You are the coach and the manager: scout talent, sign players, plan training and prepare every match.

On match day you draft champions, set the game plan and give player instructions, then watch the game unfold like a live esports broadcast with a director camera and Turkish or English casters. Read the match and play tactic cards such as Go for Aurora, Hunt Vesper, Siege, Invade or Timeout at the right moment.

Inspired by Anatolian mythology and set in the Twilight Valley, the season takes you from the Rising League to the Apex League. It is a sports management and strategy game you can play offline, in English or Turkish.

Moba Manager: frequently asked questions

Do I control the players in Moba Manager?

No. You are the coach and manager. You build the roster, set tactics and play tactic cards while the match plays out like a broadcast.

Is Moba Manager free?

Yes. It is free on Google Play, with ads and optional in-app purchases.

Can I play Moba Manager offline?

Yes. The game is playable offline.

Which languages are available?

English and Turkish, including the match commentary.
